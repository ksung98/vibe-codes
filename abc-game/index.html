Coping Skill Buddy

A supportive practice tool for distraction-based coping skills. Play the ABC Game with a calm, structured buddy.

Coping Skill Games

Choose a game, follow the prompts, and focus on one small step at a time.

How it works: In the ABC Game, move from A to Z by naming something in a category for each letter. In Take Turns with Buddy mode, one side does one letter and the other side does the next. In solo mode, the buddy guides the next prompt and tracks progress.
