Mindful Eating

Move slowly through a bite of food by noticing what your senses, body, and memories observe along the way.

Step 1 of 5 Observe
1
Observe the food

Notice the food as it is

Before eating, pause and look at the food or meal in front of you. You do not have to change anything. Just notice.

    Gentle reminders

    This activity can be done with one bite, a snack, or a meal. There is no right answer. The goal is to notice slowly.

    Go slowly Pause between steps. Let the food stay where it is until the next prompt.
    Use curiosity Notice texture, temperature, smell, shape, color, memories, and any changes over time.
    Stay kind If your mind wanders, gently return to the next sensory detail you can notice.
    Stop if needed If eating feels uncomfortable or unsafe, pause the activity and choose a different grounding tool.

    Tip: Try this with a raisin, cracker, piece of fruit, sip of tea, or the first bite of a meal.
